Rules & Terms of the Game

👑 Admin Control
The room creator acts as Admin, sets the required player count (2, 3, or 4), and starts the match once players enter.
🚫 Locked Dice Rolling
Dice rolling is locked until the Admin starts the game and all players are placed on the board.
🎲 Realtime Turn Rotation
Each player rolls on their turn. When a player moves, all other connected laptops view the movement in real time.
🐍 Snakes & 🪜 Ladders
Stepping on a snake head slides you down to its tail. Stepping on a ladder foot climbs you straight to the top!
🎯 Exact 100 to Win
You must roll the exact number to land on tile 100. The first player to reach 100 wins the match trophy!
🎨 Color Selection
Every player can choose their custom token color from the palette, visible to all players in the arena.

Rules of Engagement & Arena Protocol

👑 Executive Host Authority
The match creator acts as Admin, sets player capacity (2, 3, or 4), and initiates the game once all players have entered.
🚫 Synchronized Rolling
Dice interaction is securely locked until the Admin commences the match and each tactician occupies tile 1.
🎲 Realtime Turn Transmission
Players roll sequentially on their designated turn. Every hop and slide broadcasts instantly across all connected screens.
🐍 Serpents & 🪜 Ascents
Stepping on a serpent head forces an immediate slide to its tail. Stepping on a ladder base grants an immediate ascent to the summit.
🎯 Exact Century Rule
Victory demands an exact roll to land squarely on tile 100. Overrolls remain stationed at the current tile until the next turn.
⚜️ Distinct Heraldry
Each player selects a signature crest and tailored formal color from the palette, rendered clearly for all participants.
